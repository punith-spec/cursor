# ISB PM Capstone PPT Draft (5 Slides)

## Slide 1: Product Overview — Rakuten Smart Catalog Assistant
- Role context: VP Product, Rakuten India (global product and innovation hub)
- Product definition: AI-powered B2B catalog and merchant tooling suite
- Problem solved: low-quality listings, manual effort, compliance errors, slow go-live
- Year 1 outcomes targeted:
  - Attribute completeness: 78% -> 95%
  - Listing time per SKU: -40%
  - CTR: +10%
  - GMV uplift: +3-5%

## Slide 2: Market Opportunity & Target Customers
- Why now: catalog quality directly impacts discoverability, trust, and conversion
- Merchant pain points:
  - fragmented data and inconsistent attributes
  - limited SEO/content capability
  - manual, error-prone workflows
- Target segments:
  - Tier-1 strategic merchants (10k+ SKUs)
  - Mid-market digital-first brands (500-10k SKUs)
  - Long-tail small sellers (<=500 SKUs)
- Strategic value for Rakuten: retention, differentiation, premium monetization

## Slide 3: Strategy, Roadmap & GTM
- 3-phase roadmap:
  - Alpha (internal): model training + ops validation
  - Beta (50 merchants): A/B tests in 2 categories
  - Scaled rollout: more categories/regions + premium launch
- Positioning statement:
  - "AI partner for clean, complete, conversion-ready listings"
- Pricing:
  - Basic (free), Pro ($99/month indicative), Enterprise (custom)
- GTM channels:
  - in-product prompts, account managers, webinars, case studies

## Slide 4: AI Architecture & Success Metrics
- AI components:
  - GenAI/NLP for title/description optimization
  - Computer vision for attribute extraction
  - Policy guardrails for compliance checks
  - Ranking-aware recommendations using search/reco signals
- KPI framework:
  - Business: GMV uplift, premium revenue, retention
  - Efficiency: time-to-list, edit rate, approval turnaround
  - Model quality: acceptance rate, extraction accuracy, hallucination/violation rate
  - Experience: CSAT/NPS, engagement depth, support ticket trend

## Slide 5: Risks, Governance & Next Steps
- Key risks:
  - model inaccuracies in edge categories
  - over-automation without merchant trust
  - compliance sensitivity in regulated products
- Mitigations:
  - human-in-the-loop review for sensitive flows
  - phased rollout with strict quality thresholds
  - continuous monitoring and retraining loops
- Next steps (post-launch):
  - expand to new categories/regions
  - deepen merchant-level personalization
  - integrate with pricing, ads, and promotion tools
