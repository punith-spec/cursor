## 1. Project Overview and Objectives

As VP of Product at Rakuten India, I am leading the launch of **Rakuten Smart Catalog Assistant (SCA)**, an AI-powered merchant tool that improves product data quality and listing performance across Rakuten marketplaces. The primary objective is to help merchants create high-quality, discoverable, and compliant catalog entries with minimal manual effort, thereby improving shopper experience and increasing GMV for Rakuten Group.

SCA addresses a persistent bottleneck in marketplace growth: low-quality or incomplete product data slows merchant onboarding, hurts search relevance, and reduces conversion. By embedding AI-assisted catalog workflows into the merchant console, Rakuten can reduce listing friction while improving marketplace trust and discoverability.

### Year-1 Business Objectives

1. Increase **catalog data completeness** (mandatory attributes filled) from **78% to 95%** for active merchants.  
2. Improve **search and recommendation CTR** on product detail pages by **10%**.  
3. Reduce **merchant listing time per SKU** by **40%** through automation.  
4. Deliver **3-5% GMV uplift** attributable to improved catalog quality and conversion.

## 2. Product Concept, Market Opportunity and Target Segments

### 2.1 Product Concept

Rakuten Smart Catalog Assistant is a cloud-based module integrated into Rakuten’s merchant console. It uses AI to:

- Suggest optimized **titles, bullet points, and descriptions** using category, brand, and shopper query signals.
- Auto-fill and validate **attributes** (size, color, material, compatibility, etc.) from images, PDFs, and feed data.
- Detect **policy and compliance risks** before publishing (restricted terms, missing disclosures, unsupported claims).
- Provide **listing quality scores** and prioritized recommendations (for example: “add lifestyle image,” “clarify compatibility,” “complete material details”).

SCA is designed as a tiered offering:

- **Basic (Free):** baseline assistance for all merchants.
- **Pro:** higher automation, bulk operations, and integrations.
- **Enterprise:** APIs, governance, SLAs, and custom controls for strategic accounts.

### 2.2 Market Opportunity

Marketplace performance depends on catalog quality. Poor attributes, weak titles, and inconsistent media reduce discoverability and conversion while increasing support load and moderation effort. Across Rakuten’s merchant base, common pain points include:

- Fragmented product data across systems and channels.
- Limited in-house SEO/content capability.
- High manual effort for listing creation and maintenance.
- Rejections and delays due to policy issues.

SCA represents a strategic opportunity to:

- Improve merchant outcomes and retention.
- Differentiate Rakuten through superior seller tooling, not only fee structures.
- Build a monetizable merchant productivity layer through premium tiers.

### 2.3 Target Customer Segments

1. **Tier-1 Strategic Merchants (10k+ SKUs):**  
   Need high-scale operations, governance, and cross-region consistency.

2. **Mid-Market Digital Brands (500-10k SKUs):**  
   Need faster onboarding and quality improvements with lean content teams.

3. **Long-Tail Sellers (<=500 SKUs):**  
   Need simple guided listing flows and low-friction automation.

## 3. Strategic Development Plan

### 3.1 Discovery and Testing Plan

#### Discovery

- Conduct qualitative research with **20-30 merchants** across segments to map workflows, bottlenecks, and pain points.
- Analyze baseline catalog metrics (missing attributes, rejection rates, time-to-approval, conversion correlations).
- Benchmark against competitor and internal tooling to identify differentiated opportunity areas.

#### Phased Testing

**Phase 1: Alpha (Internal, 3 months)**  
- Train and validate models using historical catalog data.  
- Pilot internal catalog ops teams in Electronics and Fashion.  
- Track: suggestion accuracy, manual override rate, and per-SKU time saved.

**Phase 2: Beta (Merchant Pilot, 3 months)**  
- Onboard **50 merchants** (Tier-1 + mid-market mix).  
- Run A/B tests vs control merchants without SCA.  
- Track: time-to-list, completeness, approval speed, CTR/conversion shifts.

**Phase 3: Scaled Rollout (6-9 months)**  
- Expand categories and markets in waves.  
- Launch premium plans and refine UX based on in-product feedback loops.

### 3.2 Positioning Strategy

**Positioning Statement:**  
“Rakuten Smart Catalog Assistant is the AI partner that keeps product listings clean, complete, and conversion-ready, so merchants can focus on selling.”

**Positioning Pillars**

1. **Accuracy + Compliance:** trusted guardrails that reduce publishing risk.  
2. **Productivity:** measurable reduction in listing effort and rework.  
3. **Growth:** improved listing quality tied to discoverability and sales.

**Differentiation**

- Native integration with Rakuten ranking, recommendation, and policy systems.
- Category-aware optimization tuned to Rakuten marketplace dynamics.
- Backed by Rakuten India’s cross-domain capability in ecommerce, data, and AI.

### 3.3 Pricing Strategy

- **Basic (Free):** single-SKU assistance, limited scoring and fixes.
- **Pro (~US$99/account/month):** bulk optimization, feed ingestion, richer automation.
- **Enterprise (Custom):** APIs, advanced controls, region management, SLA support.

Pricing will be validated during beta through willingness-to-pay experiments and ROI framing (time saved, errors reduced, conversion uplift).

### 3.4 Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

#### Channels

- In-product placement inside merchant console.
- Account management and merchant success teams.
- Webinars, case studies, and self-serve documentation.

#### GTM Sequence

1. **Internal enablement:** train sales/support teams with scripts and demos.  
2. **Pilot proof points:** publish 2-3 case studies with quantified outcome metrics.  
3. **Launch campaign:** “Smart Catalog Month” with free Pro trial for early adopters.  
4. **Growth loops:** in-product nudges driven by listing quality scores.

## 4. Performance Metrics and AI Integration

### 4.1 AI Integration Framework

SCA combines:

- **NLP + GenAI:** optimized listing text generation under policy constraints.
- **Computer vision:** image-derived attribute extraction and validation.
- **Ranking-aware recommendation logic:** prioritize edits with highest expected impact on CTR/conversion.

To ensure safe deployment:

- Human-in-the-loop review for sensitive categories.
- Explicit policy filters before publish.
- Drift and hallucination monitoring with rollback thresholds.

### 4.2 Success Metrics

#### A. Business Impact

- GMV uplift % (SCA cohort vs control cohort).
- Premium plan revenue and upgrade rate.
- Merchant retention and expansion metrics.

#### B. Operational Efficiency

- Time-to-list per SKU.
- Manual edit/override rate on AI output.
- Catalog approval turnaround time.

#### C. Product + Model Quality

- Suggestion acceptance rate.
- Attribute extraction precision/recall (against reviewed truth sets).
- Policy violation and hallucination rates.

#### D. Merchant Experience

- SCA CSAT/NPS.
- Repeat usage and session-level engagement.
- Support ticket volume related to listing quality.

## 5. Long-Term Recommendations

1. **Expand category and regional coverage**  
   Scale from Electronics/Fashion to Home, Grocery, and long-tail verticals with localized policy rules.

2. **Personalize recommendations by merchant context**  
   Tailor suggestions by merchant maturity, brand voice, and historical conversion patterns.

3. **Integrate with adjacent merchant tools**  
   Connect with ad, pricing, and promotion systems for unified decision support.

4. **Strengthen AI governance and trust controls**  
   Keep transparent policies, fairness checks, and auditability for sensitive categories.

5. **Institutionalize feedback loops**  
   Capture explicit merchant feedback (“helpful/not helpful”) and use it for model and UX calibration.
