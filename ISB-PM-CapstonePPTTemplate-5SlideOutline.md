# ISB PM Capstone PPT Draft
## Product: Rakuten Smart Catalog Assistant (SCA)

### Slide 1: Product Overview and Business Problem
- Rakuten Smart Catalog Assistant: AI-powered merchant catalog optimization suite.
- Problem: incomplete/inconsistent product data lowers discoverability and conversion.
- Who it serves: marketplace merchants across enterprise, mid-market, and long tail.
- Why now: scale of SKUs + rising content/compliance complexity + AI readiness.
- Year-1 objectives: completeness to 95%, listing time down 40%, CTR +10%, GMV uplift 3-5%.

### Slide 2: Market Opportunity and Target Customers
- Marketplace growth is constrained by catalog quality and merchant productivity.
- Merchant pain points: fragmented data, manual listing workflows, policy rejections.
- Segment 1: Tier-1 strategic merchants (10k+ SKUs), need APIs/governance.
- Segment 2: Mid-market brands (500-10k SKUs), need scalable automation.
- Segment 3: Long-tail sellers (<=500 SKUs), need guided, low-friction flows.

### Slide 3: Strategy, Roadmap and Go-To-Market
- Discovery: merchant interviews + baseline metric analysis + competitive benchmarking.
- Alpha (internal): model training + ops validation in Electronics/Fashion.
- Beta (50 merchants): A/B testing vs control on speed, quality, and conversion.
- Scale: category/region expansion + premium plan rollout.
- GTM: in-product promotion, account-manager-led adoption, case studies, free Pro trial.

### Slide 4: AI Approach and Success Metrics
- AI stack: GenAI for text optimization, CV for attribute extraction, policy guardrails.
- Intelligence loop: rank-aware recommendations tied to CTR/conversion impact.
- Metrics: business (GMV, premium revenue), operations (time-to-list, approval speed).
- Product/model metrics: acceptance rate, extraction accuracy, hallucination/violation rate.
- Merchant experience: CSAT/NPS, repeat usage, support ticket reduction.

### Slide 5: Risks, Governance and Next Steps
- Key risks: AI errors, category-specific edge cases, merchant trust/adoption barriers.
- Mitigations: human-in-the-loop for sensitive categories, policy filters, confidence thresholds.
- Governance: fairness monitoring, audit logs, transparent merchant controls.
- Next 12 months: expand categories/regions, personalize recommendations, integrate ads/pricing/promotions.
- Strategic outcome: create a defensible merchant tooling moat and sustained GMV growth.
