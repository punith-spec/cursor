## Slide 2: The Opportunity

# Why This Role Matters Now

CIS is at a critical scale point where ambiguity in requirements creates downstream engineering cost.

| Current Pain | Impact |
|---|---|
| High-context JP business intent gets lost across time zones | Rework in IN engineering |
| Requirements clarified late in the cycle | Slower delivery, design debt |
| No single owner for cross-functional UX decisions | Inconsistent experience |
| Design patterns vary across CIS modules | Fragmented user experience |

A dedicated **UX Architect bridge** resolves these at the source.

---

## Slide 3: The Role We Are Proposing

# Senior UX Architect — Bridge Profile

A senior practitioner who owns the **translation layer** between business intent and engineering execution.

**Core charter:**
- Convert JP business/policy requirements into clear, buildable UX architecture
- Own CIS information architecture, user flows, and design standards
- Facilitate Japan–India alignment on UX decisions
- Reduce ambiguity-driven rework

**Title options:**
- Senior UX Architect (preferred)
- Lead UX Architect — CIS
- UX Architect / Bridge (JP–IN)

## Slide 9: Deliverables for CIS Pilot

# What We Will Produce in 90 Days

1. **Business Intent Canvas** — one-page JP + EN summary of CIS business intent, constraints, and success metrics
2. **Current-State Journey Map** — end-to-end CIS journey with friction points
3. **Target UX Architecture** — IA, flows, role-based views, edge cases
4. **UX Architecture Spec** — engineering-ready specs with acceptance criteria
5. **Architecture Decision Log (ADR)** — record of major decisions, options, and trade-offs
6. **Governance Checklist** — review gate before development begins
